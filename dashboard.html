Four steps, most of them free

Detection and rehearsal run instantly with no AI involved. Only the repair step calls on Bob — and only for dependencies that actually need it.

01

Detect

Reads your manifest and lockfile, checks the npm registry and audit data, and flags every outdated, deprecated, or vulnerable dependency.

02

Rehearse

Creates an isolated git worktree per dependency, installs the target version for real, and runs your actual test suite against it.

03

Repair

For anything that broke, Bob finds where it's used, explains the real breaking change, and writes a fix — inside its own branch only.

04

Verify

The fix is re-tested against your real suite before it's ever marked safe. Nothing is called "fixed" without a rerun proving it.

What actually runs where

The expensive, deterministic work never touches an AI model. Bob is only called in for the one thing scripts can't do: understanding why something broke.
